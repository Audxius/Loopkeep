<p><b>Loopkeep keeps every TikTok you ever saved</b>, so you can delete TikTok without losing them.</p>

<p>It downloads your saved videos to your phone together with their full captions, creators and dates, into a plain folder you own. Each post becomes a Markdown note with its video and cover next to it, so the folder works inside <b>Obsidian</b> too. If the app disappears, the folder is still a complete archive.</p>

<p><b>Watch them like TikTok, minus TikTok</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Full-screen vertical feed: swipe, loop, tap to pause, seek, mute</li>
<li>Photo carousels with their sound</li>
<li>No account, no algorithm, no tracking, no infinite feed: only what you saved</li>
<li>Newest, oldest or shuffled</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Bring everything over</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Import TikTok's "Download your data" export (JSON, TXT, HTML or the ZIP)</li>
<li>Share any video, photo post or public collection to Loopkeep from TikTok</li>
<li>Downloads run in the background, politely, and resume after interruptions</li>
<li>If a video can't be downloaded, its caption and cover are still kept</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Straight from the creator</b></p>
<ul>
<li>The creator's own comments and replies under each post are saved with it</li>
<li>The link in their bio is kept, so it's there even after TikTok is gone</li>
<li>Tap the creator to open their TikTok profile</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Find and organise</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Full-text search over captions, tags, notes and creators</li>
<li>Collections, your own tags and notes</li>
<li>Filters by collection, status, tag and creator</li>
<li>Counters for saved, partial, unavailable and failed posts</li>
</ul>

<p>Everything you edit is written back into the Markdown files. Edits you make in Obsidian show up in the app.</p>

<p>Backing up is copying the folder.</p>

<p>Loopkeep is an independent project, not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance. Keep copies for yourself; respect creators' rights.</p>
